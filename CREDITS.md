## Generated Madar assets

Eight custom visual assets were generated with ChatGPT from the prompts collected in `analysis/prompts/madar-image-prompts.md` and `analysis/prompts/برومبتات_مدار_للأصول_البصرية.docx`. They were generated specifically for this project and converted to WebP under `public/assets/generated/`.

- `01-madar-hero.webp` — cinematic main hero.
- `02-login-background.webp` — login background.
- `03-madar-assistant.webp` — assistant symbol.
- `04-accounts-pages.webp` — connected accounts and pages.
- `05-conversations-inbox.webp` — unified inbox concept.
- `06-campaign-operations.webp` — campaign operations desk.
- `07-empty-guidance.webp` — empty-state guidance lantern.
- `08-mobile-hero.webp` — vertical mobile hero.

The images contain no brand logos, readable text, or copyrighted game artwork. Prompts avoid replication of specific protected characters and designs.
